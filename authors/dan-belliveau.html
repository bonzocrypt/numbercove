Founder and lead developer

Dan Belliveau

Former Fidelity Investments senior options and equities trader

Background

Dan Belliveau spent 17 years at Fidelity Investments (1998–2015), moving from trading and service specialist to equities and options trader, then active-trader product development, and finally senior trader on the Active Trader Services desk, where he approved large equity and options orders, led a team of 13 representatives, and wrote specifications for trading platforms. He previously held FINRA Series 3, 4, 7, 9, 10, 55, and 63 registrations and is a licensed Florida real estate sales associate. He now builds software and financial tools.

How the calculators are built

Every calculator states its formula, cites the public sources behind its default values, and lets you change each assumption. Core math is tested against published examples before release, and tools are reviewed when the underlying rules change, such as contribution limits, carrier pricing rules, or tax law.
