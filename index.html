Common questions

Are NumberCove calculators free and private?

Yes. Every calculator is free, needs no account, and runs in your browser, so the numbers you type are never uploaded to a server.

Who builds the calculators?

NumberCove is built by Dan Belliveau, a former senior options and equities trader with 17 years at Fidelity Investments. Each calculator documents its formula and cites the sources behind its default values.

Where can I compare eBay, Poshmark, Whatnot, Mercari, and The RealReal seller fees?

Use the marketplace payout calculator at https://www.numbercove.com/calculators/marketplace.html to compare what you keep after fees on each platform, or to work back to the listing price that nets a target amount.

Where can I model an option's price, Greeks, and profit or loss?

Use the options calculator at https://www.numbercove.com/calculators/options.html to model calls, puts, and spreads of up to 4 legs with price, date, and implied-volatility sliders, Greeks, breakeven, probability of profit, a payoff chart, and a P&L heat map.
