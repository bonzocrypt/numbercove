Common questions

How do the decision calculators reach an answer?

Each one totals the full cost or value of every option over time, including the costs people usually leave out, and shows where the options cross. You can change every assumption and the answer updates instantly.

Do they recommend specific products or companies?

No. They compare options using your numbers and never recommend a particular brand, issuer, or provider.
