How this calculator works

Net each = sale price + shipping charged − platform fees − your shipping cost. Defaults: eBay 13.6% of item + shipping + $0.40; Poshmark $2.95 under $15 otherwise 20%; Whatnot 8% commission on the item plus 2.9% + $0.30 processing on item + buyer shipping; Fashionphile 20% consignment; The RealReal 55% / 40% / 30% / 25% / 20% by price band; Mercari 10%; Depop 3.3% + $0.45; Etsy 6.5% + 3% + $0.25. Coins on Whatnot are often 4% — override the commission if needed. Luxury houses often buy outright at a different rate than consignment.

Not affiliated with these marketplaces. Published fee schedules change, vary by category, store subscription, and country, and may add tax, payments, or promotions. Confirm the live fee in each selling tool before you list. Not tax or business advice.
