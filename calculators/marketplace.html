How this calculator works

By default, enter your listing price and each row shows what you will get after estimated platform fees and any postage you enter under More options. Choose “This is what I want to make” to work backward: each row then shows the listing price that leaves you at least your target amount. Vinted US charges sellers no fee, so with no postage of your own its payout matches your listing price. The buyer pays a Vinted fee, usually 5% + $0.70, plus shipping. Fashionphile and The RealReal show the price the item needs to sell for. Optional Vinted Item Bumps are not included.

Other U.S. charts: eBay most categories 13.6% of item + shipping up to $7,500 then 2.35%, plus $0.30 on orders of $10 or less and $0.40 above (handbags 15% at $2,000 or less and 9% above, jewelry 15% at $5,000 or less and 9% above, watches 15% to $1,000 then 6.5% to $7,500 then 3%, athletic shoes 8% at $150+ with no per-order fee, coins and cards 13.25%). Poshmark is $2.95 under $15 and 20% at $15+. Whatnot is 8% on the item (4% for coins, 8% on the first $1,500 for cards) plus 2.9% + $0.30. Mercari is 10% of item + buyer shipping. Fashionphile is 30% on the first $3,000 and 15% above. The RealReal pays a category share of the sold price. Set your status under More options. Trendsetter is the default, and VIP keeps more on items $200+. Fashionphile and The RealReal are included for clothing, handbags, jewelry, watches, and sneakers. Vinted is included for every category. Luxury houses often buy outright at a different rate than consignment.

Not affiliated with these marketplaces. Published fee schedules change, vary by category, store subscription, and country, and may add tax, payments, or promotions. Confirm the live fee in each selling tool before you list. Not tax or business advice.
