How this calculator works

Net each = sale price + shipping charged − platform fees − your shipping cost. Pick an item type to load the matching U.S. charts: eBay most categories 13.6% of item + shipping up to $7,500 then 2.35% (handbags 15%/9%, jewelry 15%/9%, watches 15%/6.5%/3%, athletic shoes 8% at $150+, coins and cards 13.25%); Poshmark $2.95 under $15 otherwise 20%; Whatnot 8% on the item (4% coins; TCG/sports/comics 8% on the first $1,500) plus 2.9% + $0.30 processing on checkout; Fashionphile 30% consignment on the first $3,000 and 15% above; The RealReal pays a category share of the sold price (clothing 20–70%, higher on bags/jewelry/watches/sneakers). VIP is the highest RealReal Rewards tier: you keep more than a Trendsetter on items $200+ — for example 60% vs 55% on a $250 bag, 80% vs 75% on a $5,000 bag. Mercari 10% of item + buyer shipping. Choose All platforms to compare, or one venue to see its extra fields (eBay promoted listings, The RealReal status). Fashionphile and The RealReal appear for luxury categories only. Luxury houses often buy outright at a different rate than consignment.
