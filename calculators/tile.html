Tile estimator

Counts tiles along each wall using tile size plus grout, then adds waste for cuts and breakage. Better than a raw square-footage guess for rectangular layouts.

By Dan Belliveau · Former Fidelity Investments senior options and equities traderUpdated Aug 24, 2026 · Free, private, runs in your browser

How this calculator works

Tiles along a side = ceil((room + grout) / (tile + grout)). Total = columns x rows, then increased by the waste percent and rounded up. Irregular rooms, niches, and diagonal layouts need more waste than 10%.

The row-and-column method accounts for grout joints and for the fact that a cut piece at one edge may not fit the opposite edge. After the layout count is calculated, the waste allowance covers breakage, color matching, future repairs, and unusable cuts. Box count is always rounded up to a whole box.

Measure the actual tiled surface rather than the room footprint when working on walls, backsplashes, showers, or stairs. Subtract large openings only when doing so will not leave you short. Tile dimensions can be nominal rather than exact, and modular patterns, borders, offsets, mosaics, and diagonal installations should be planned from a drawing or dry layout before ordering.

Does not include thinset, grout volume, or edge profiles. Keep extra from the same caliber and shade.
