How this calculator works

Working backward

Start with what the buyer pays (item plus any shipping they're charged), subtract platform fees, promoted-listing fees, the shipping label, supplies, a returns allowance, and the value of your time. What's left must cover the item cost and your profit.

Net = buyer pays − fees − promotion − label − supplies − returns − time

Targets

  • Return on cost: max cost = net ÷ (1 + ROI).
  • Profit margin: max cost = net − margin × sale price.
  • Dollar profit: max cost = net − target.

If you pay sales tax when you buy, the max price before tax is max cost ÷ (1 + tax rate).

Platform fees used

  • eBay (most categories): 13.6% of item plus shipping up to $7,500 (2.35% above), plus $0.30 per order of $10 or less or $0.40 above.
  • Poshmark: $2.95 under $15, otherwise 20%.
  • Mercari: 10% of item plus buyer shipping.
  • Whatnot: 8% commission plus 2.9% + $0.30 processing.
  • Etsy: 6.5% transaction fee plus 3% + $0.25 payment processing and a $0.20 listing fee.
  • Vinted: no seller fee.

Category-specific rates differ; see the marketplace payout calculator for category detail, or choose Custom.

Common questions

What is a good ROI for reselling?

Many resellers aim for at least 2x to 3x their cost (100% to 200% return) on low-priced items to cover time and misses, and accept lower percentage returns on higher-priced items where the dollar profit is larger. Set the target that fits your time and volume.

Should I include my time?

If you're deciding whether a flip is worth doing at all, yes. Enter minutes per item and an hourly value, and the calculator lowers your max buy price accordingly.

How do I estimate the sale price?

Use recent sold listings for the same item and condition, not active asking prices. Plug in a conservative number; the chart shows how your max price changes if it sells for more or less.
