How this calculator works

Wall area = 2 x (length + width) x height, minus 21 sq ft per door and 15 sq ft per window. Optional ceiling adds length x width. Gallons = (area x coats) / coverage, then rounded up to a whole gallon for purchasing.

Texture, primer, dark colors, and cheap rollers all change real coverage. Check the can and buy a little extra from the same lot.
