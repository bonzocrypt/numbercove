How this calculator works

Net value

Net value = Σ spend × earn rate × point value + Σ credits × share you use − annual fee

Credits count only at the share you'd really use. A credit that replaces spending you'd do anyway is worth its face value; one that pushes you to buy something you wouldn't is worth much less.

Break-even spending

Rewards grow with spending while the fee and credits are fixed. The break-even spend is where both cards net the same, assuming your spending mix stays the same:

Break-even spend = (alternative's fixed value − this card's fixed value) ÷ (this card's reward rate − alternative's rate)

Point value

Cash back is 1¢ per point. Points transferred to travel partners can be worth more, but only if you book those redemptions. The calculator also shows the point value at which the two cards tie.

Common questions

How do I know if a credit card annual fee is worth it?

Add up the rewards you'll earn at a realistic point value and the credits you'd actually use, subtract the fee, and compare that with what a no-fee card would earn on the same spending.

Should I downgrade or cancel a card with an annual fee?

A downgrade to a no-fee card from the same issuer usually keeps your account history and credit limit, which helps your credit utilization and account age. Canceling ends both.

Does this calculator recommend specific cards?

No. It compares the values you enter and doesn't recommend any issuer or product.
