How this calculator works

Metric BMI = weight (kg) / height (m)^2. US BMI = 703 x weight (lb) / height (in)^2. Categories follow WHO cutoffs: underweight below 18.5, healthy 18.5-24.9, overweight 25-29.9, obesity 30+. The healthy-weight range is the body weight that would place BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 at the same height.

BMI does not distinguish muscle from fat and can misclassify athletes, older adults, and some ethnic groups. It is not a diagnosis. Talk with a clinician about your health.
